About EcoBite — Our Story

A Small Idea.
A Bigger Impact.

Every day, perfectly good food is left unused after events, restaurant operations, and social gatherings. At the same time, many individuals and families face food insecurity.

EcoBite was created to help bridge this gap. By using technology to connect food donors with verified NGOs and delivery partners, we aim to make surplus food redistribution simpler, faster, and more accessible.

Our goal is to turn food waste into an opportunity to support people and protect the environment.

Guided By Purpose

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To reduce avoidable food waste by making surplus food donation easy, accessible, and efficient while helping communities facing food insecurity.

Our Vision

To build a future where surplus food reaches people in need instead of ending up in landfills.

Addressing Challenges

The Problem We're Solving

1. Food Waste

Large quantities of edible food are discarded despite opportunities for redistribution.

2. Food Insecurity

Many communities struggle to access sufficient nutritious food on a daily basis.

3. Connection Gap

Food donors and charitable organizations often lack an efficient way to connect in real time.

Seamless Process

How EcoBite Works

1

Post Surplus Food

Hotels, restaurants, and event organizers submit details about available surplus food.

2

Discover Donations

NGOs explore available food donations and identify suitable opportunities nearby.

3

Coordinate Pickup

Donors, NGOs, and delivery partners coordinate the collection and delivery process.

4

Create Impact

Surplus food is redirected toward communities in need instead of being wasted.

Our Network

Who Can Join EcoBite?

Restaurants & Hotels

Post surplus buffet items, prepared meals, and unserved inventory quickly.

Event Organizers

Avoid post-wedding or banquet food waste by scheduling bulk meal pickups.

NGOs & Shelters

Browse real-time available food listings and request pickup for local communities.

Delivery Partners

Volunteer or assist in transporting meals safely from donors to partner organizations.
