Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

1. What is EcoBite?
EcoBite is a community-driven web platform that connects restaurants, hotels, and event hosts with local NGOs and delivery volunteers to redistribute edible surplus food quickly and safely.
2. How can I donate surplus food?
Simply sign up as a Donor, navigate to "Donate Food", select your food category and quantity, specify your pickup address, and submit. Nearby NGOs will receive real-time alerts.
3. Can NGOs register on EcoBite?
Yes! Verified NGOs and community shelters can register as an NGO account to browse real-time available food listings in their area and claim donations instantly.
4. Is EcoBite free to use?
Yes, EcoBite is completely free for food donors and charitable organizations to connect and redistribute surplus food.
5. How are food pickups coordinated?
Once an NGO claims a donation, pickup arrangements can be managed directly or assigned to registered delivery partners for timely transport.
6. How can I become a delivery partner?
Select "Delivery" during sign up to join as a delivery volunteer or partner driver to assist in transporting claimed meals safely.
7. How can I report a problem?
You can send us a message directly using the contact form on this page or email our support team at support@ecobite.com.
